How it started

We saw great brands
being let down.

MarkWings didn't start in a boardroom. It started with a frustration — watching genuinely brilliant brands get mediocre treatment because the marketing world had fragmented itself into too many specialists who never spoke to each other. The social team didn't know the design brief. The designers didn't understand the marketing strategy. The developer built whatever they were told, not what the brand actually needed.

So we set out to build something different. A studio where social, design, development, and video aren't departments — they're instruments in the same band, playing the same song for your brand. Every decision, every pixel, every post — connected and intentional.

"Your brand has a story worth telling. We exist to make the world listen."

What we do

01

Social Media Management

Daily conversations, campaigns, and content that build communities and convert followers into customers. We manage your voice so it always sounds like you — but better. Consistent, strategic, and impossible to scroll past.

02

Web Development

Fast, beautiful, conversion-optimised websites and web apps. We build digital homes that are as impressive to look at as they are to use — and that Google wants to rank first. Performance and aesthetics, never at the cost of each other.

03

Graphic Design

Logos, brand identities, packaging, and everything in between. Visual storytelling crafted to stop the scroll, anchor the brand, and stay in memory long after the first look. We design for recognition — and for reverence.

04

Video Editing

Reels, teasers, brand films, and campaign content edited to move people — emotionally, and towards that CTA. We take your raw footage and turn it into scroll-stopping stories that make your audience feel something, then act on it.
